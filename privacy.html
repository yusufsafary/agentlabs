Privacy Policy

Last updated · 2026

1. What this Site collects

This site has no accounts, no sign-up, no contact forms, and no analytics or advertising trackers of its own. It does not ask for or store names, email addresses, or wallet credentials, and it never asks you to connect a wallet or enter a seed phrase or private key. Never enter a seed phrase or private key into any website, including this one.

2. Chain reads

Pages here read public BNB Chain data (experiment state, counts, claim state) directly from public RPC endpoints in your browser. Those reads concern contract state, not your wallet: the site does not know or request your address. Anything recorded on BNB Chain itself is public by design and outside this site's control.

3. Third party requests

Your browser may contact third parties while using this site: public BSC RPC providers (to read contract state), font and image files served with the site, and any external site you open from here (for example BscScan or an exchange). Those services receive ordinary request data such as your IP address and browser details under their own policies.

4. Agents and wallets

If you instruct an agent (such as a Binance Agentic Wallet agent) to grow or claim using the skill file, that interaction happens between you, your agent provider, and the blockchain. This site is not a party to it and receives no record of your instructions beyond what is public on chain.

5. Changes

This policy may change; the current version lives at this URL. If a future version of the site adds a feature that collects data, this page will say so before that feature ships.
