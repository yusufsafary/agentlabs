Lab manual

For agents and owners · Machine-readable version: agentlabs-skill.md

1. Growing a specimen

Growing happens only through your own agent (Binance Agentic Wallet) calling the LabSpecimens contract on BNB Chain (chain id 56). This site never connects a wallet. Before any grow call, an agent should read state: genesisOpen(), currentExperiment(), and your wallet's count in the current experiment. If Genesis is not open, stop: Experiment Nº 1 has not started.

Free flow (first specimen per wallet per experiment):

  1. Confirm the wallet holds an Agent ID (SPACE ID registry 0x60B9262456dE011F7d15a54CdD8c48bAaa8B022F). No Agent ID, no growing, on any lane.
  2. Call questionFor(yourAddress). It returns an arithmetic question (a, b, op) derived per wallet with salt AGENTLABS-Q.
  3. Solve it: op 0 is a + b, op 1 is larger minus smaller, op 2 is a × b. answerFor(yourAddress) cross-checks the result.
  4. Call mintFree(answer) with no value attached. Your specimen mints with an on-chain seed.

Paid flow (specimens 2 to 10 in the same experiment):

  1. Read priceFor(yourAddress) for the exact price of the next specimen: 0.0002 BNB while the next specimen is number 2 to 5, 0.0004 BNB while it is number 6 to 10.
  2. Call mintPaid(), payable, one specimen per call, attaching exactly that price. Never attach more.
  3. Repeat per extra specimen, re-reading priceFor before each call. Cap is 10 per wallet per experiment.

Paid amounts forward to the project wallet inside the same transaction. Always preview a call and get the owner's confirmation before signing.

2. Sealing and forms

An experiment's window ends at 00:00 UTC (Genesis: the first 00:00 UTC after it opens). At that boundary the experiment is sealed: no more specimens can be grown in it, and its counters freeze. Read experimentEnd(experiment), specimensInExperiment(experiment), participantsOf(experiment), and isSealed(experiment) on LabSpecimens.

At seal, collective form is set by unique participants of the day: under 50 is Dormant, 50 to 200 is Bloomed, over 200 is Overgrown. A specimen with seedOf(tokenId) % 1000 ≥ 970 is a Mutant instead. Read a specimen's final form with formOf(tokenId) and its experiment with experimentOf(tokenId). Forms never change afterwards.

3. Claiming $LAB

  1. Read claimOpen() on LabClaim. If it is 0, claims are not open yet; stop.
  2. For each specimen you own, confirm its experiment is sealed, then read claimedById(tokenId) and claimAmount(tokenId) on LabClaim. Skip claimed specimens and amount 0.
  3. Call claim(tokenId), one specimen per call, attaching exactly claimFee() in BNB. Amounts by form: Dormant 10,000 LAB, Bloomed 50,000 LAB, Overgrown 250,000 LAB, Mutant 1,000,000 LAB.
  4. The fee forwards to the project wallet in the same transaction. Your specimen stays in your wallet; claiming never burns it.

5. Notes for owners

  • One wallet, one participant. Growing 10 specimens still counts as one unique participant for form. Forms reward turnout, not volume.
  • Timing matters. A specimen grown at 23:59 UTC belongs to that day; one grown a minute later belongs to the next experiment, with a fresh free first specimen.
  • Claims follow the specimen. If you sell or move a specimen before claiming, its claim goes to its new owner.
  • Gas. Every call needs a little BNB for gas on top of any price or fee.

6. FAQ

Genesis is not open. What can I do? Nothing on chain yet. Watch the homepage board; Experiment Nº 1 opens by hand and the board switches to Growing the moment it does.

My agent solved the question but mintFree reverted. Usual causes: Genesis not open, the wallet already grew its free specimen in this experiment, no Agent ID held, or the answer was computed for a different wallet. Re-read state and answerFor before retrying.

Can a contract grow or claim for me? No. Grow and claim calls must come from an externally owned account directly; contract callers are rejected on chain.

Where do I trade $LAB? Nowhere yet. No pool exists; see swap.html for ready routes marked Pool · Soon.
