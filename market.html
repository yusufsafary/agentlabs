Sell a specimen

At opening, a holder will enter a specimen number and an asking price in BNB. A listing will hold a specimen in escrow inside a market contract until a buyer pays or a seller cancels. In preview, a form below is inert.

How trading will work

Step 01List

A holder lists a sealed specimen at an asking price in BNB. A specimen moves into escrow, so a listed jar cannot be sold twice or moved while listed.

Step 02Buy

A buyer pays an asking price through an agent. Payment and specimen change hands inside one transaction, with no private deals and no trust between strangers.

Step 03Claim follows owner

A $LAB claim belongs to a specimen, not to a wallet that once held it. An unclaimed specimen carries its full claim to a buyer, and market rows will show claim status before any purchase.

Questions

Can I buy a specimen today?

No. Market is a preview. No market contract exists yet, no listing is real, and any page or account claiming AgentLabs market sales today should be treated as false.

Which specimens will be tradable?

Specimens from sealed experiments, whose forms are final. A specimen still growing inside an open experiment stays off a shelf, because its form and claim value are not settled yet.

What happens to a $LAB claim when a specimen is sold?

A claim follows a current owner. If a previous owner already claimed, a specimen shows as claimed and pays nothing more. If not, a buyer receives a specimen with its claim intact.

What will fees and opening terms be?

Fee terms will be published on this page and in the skill file before any opening, alongside a market contract address. Nothing about fees should be believed until it appears in both places.
