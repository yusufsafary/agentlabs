$LAB tokenomics

Fixed supply · No presale · Figures below are contract rules, not targets

Supply

$LAB (contract name LabToken) has a fixed supply of 10,000,000,000 LAB, created once at deployment. Its contract has no mint function, so supply can never grow. No $LAB was sold in a presale, and no allocation exists for advisors, exchanges, or a public sale.

Claim pool. 2,000,000,000 LAB (20% of supply) funds holder claims. It is held by the LabClaim contract and paid out only through per-specimen claims at the fixed amounts below. Pool status · Funding at deployment Live pool balance is readable on the homepage claim board once contracts are live.

Project wallet. 8,000,000,000 LAB (80% of supply) sits in the project wallet, 0x7f2ea716f5CDe280599F1928c30Ca308aD496a05. Any future use, such as liquidity for a trading pool, will be visible on chain from this address.

Claim amounts per form

Every specimen of a sealed experiment claims $LAB once, paid by its final form. Amounts are fixed in the LabClaim contract:

Claiming never burns or moves a specimen. One specimen claims once, ever, paid to whoever owns it at claim time.

Fee mechanics

  • Claim fee: each claim call attaches a flat fee in BNB, set as an immutable value in the LabClaim contract and forwarded to the project wallet inside the same transaction. The LabClaim contract keeps no BNB balance.
  • Paid specimens: specimens 2 to 5 cost 0.0002 BNB each and specimens 6 to 10 cost 0.0004 BNB each. Payments forward to the project wallet inside the same transaction; the LabSpecimens contract keeps no balance.
  • Token transfers: $LAB itself carries no transfer tax in its contract.

Pool status

No $LAB liquidity pool exists yet, so $LAB cannot be swapped today. Swap routes on this site are prefilled and honestly marked Pool · Soon. If a pool is created, it will be funded from the project wallet allocation and announced on the homepage and the swap page. See swap.html.

$LAB is not sold by this site, carries no promise of price, yield, or buyback, and is not a security or an investment product. Read the Disclaimer before interacting with any AgentLabs contract.
