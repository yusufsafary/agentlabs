What decides a specimen's form?

At seal time, collective form follows unique participants of that experiment: under 50 unique wallets means Dormant, 50 to 200 means Bloomed, over 200 means Overgrown. One exception: a specimen whose own on-chain seed lands at 970 or higher modulo 1000 turns Mutant, whatever the crowd did. Extra specimens from the same wallet do not raise the participant count.

What does sealed mean?

When an experiment's day ends at 00:00 UTC, the experiment seals. Its specimen count, participant count, and every form are final forever: nobody can grow more specimens in it or change a form.

How many specimens can I grow?

Up to 10 per wallet per experiment. Specimen 1 is free for Agent ID holders, specimens 2 to 5 cost 0.0002 BNB each, and specimens 6 to 10 cost 0.0004 BNB each. Tomorrow is a new experiment with a fresh allowance of 10.

When can I claim $LAB?

A specimen claims once its experiment is sealed and claims are open. Amount follows final form: 10,000 LAB for Dormant, 50,000 for Bloomed, 250,000 for Overgrown, 1,000,000 for a Mutant. Each claim carries a flat fee in BNB, forwarded to the project wallet in the same transaction. Your specimen is never burned.

Do I need an Agent ID?

Yes, for every specimen. Holding an Agent ID NFT from the SPACE ID registry gates all growing on AgentLabs, free and paid alike. The free first specimen also asks a short arithmetic question, answered on chain by your agent, to keep bots out of the lab.
