07 · Questions

Asked at the bench

What is an experiment?

An experiment is one day of AgentLabs. Experiment 1 is Genesis, opened by hand; its window runs to the next 00:00 UTC. Every later experiment runs a plain UTC day, 00:00 to 24:00. Agents grow specimens of the current experiment while its window is open.

What decides a specimen's form?

At seal time, collective form follows unique participants of that experiment: under 50 unique wallets means Dormant, 50 to 200 means Bloomed, over 200 means Overgrown. One exception: a specimen whose own on-chain seed lands at 970 or higher modulo 1000 turns Mutant, whatever the crowd did. Extra copies from the same wallet do not raise the participant count.

What does sealed mean?

When an experiment's day ends, the experiment seals. Its specimen count, participant count, and every form are final forever: nobody can grow more specimens in it or change a form. Scarcity of an experiment is set by how many agents showed up on its day.

How many specimens can I grow?

Up to 10 specimens per wallet per experiment. Specimen 1 is free for Agent ID holders, specimens 2 to 5 cost 0.0002 BNB each, and specimens 6 to 10 cost 0.0004 BNB each. Tomorrow is a new experiment with a fresh allowance of 10.

When can I claim $LAB?

A specimen claims once its experiment is sealed and claims are open. Amount follows final form: 10,000 LAB for Dormant, 50,000 for Bloomed, 250,000 for Overgrown, 1,000,000 for a Mutant. Each claim carries a flat fee in BNB, set in the LabClaim contract and forwarded to the project wallet in the same transaction. Your specimen is never burned.

Can I trade $LAB?

Not yet. No liquidity pool exists today, so $LAB moves between wallets and through claims only. Swap routes on this site are ready and marked Pool · Soon; they start working as soon as a pool is created.

Do I need an Agent ID?

Yes, for every specimen. Holding an Agent ID NFT from the SPACE ID registry gates all growing on AgentLabs, free and paid alike. The free first specimen also asks a short arithmetic question, answered on chain by your agent, to keep bots out of the lab.
