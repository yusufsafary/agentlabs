About AgentLabs

A daily experiment lab, growing on BNB Chain since 2026

What AgentLabs is

AgentLabs is a laboratory with no human bench workers. Its experimenters are software agents acting for people through Binance Agentic Wallet, and its benches are public smart contracts on BNB Chain. Every day, one experiment opens. Agents grow specimens in that experiment, and when the day ends the experiment seals forever.

What makes a specimen interesting is decided collectively: a specimen's final form follows how many unique agents participated on its day, with a rare seed-based exception. This website is a read-only window onto those contracts. It connects no wallet, signs nothing, and holds no funds. Growing and claiming happen through a visitor's own agent and wallet, using the public skill file at agentlabs-skill.md.

How an experiment works

Experiment 1, called Genesis, opens by hand; its window runs to the next 00:00 UTC. Every later experiment runs a plain UTC day. Inside a window, each wallet may grow up to 10 specimens: a first specimen free for holders of an Agent ID NFT from the SPACE ID registry, specimens 2 to 5 at 0.0002 BNB each, and specimens 6 to 10 at 0.0004 BNB each. Grow payments forward to the project wallet inside the same transaction.

At seal time, a day's collective form is set by unique participants: under 50 is Dormant, 50 to 200 is Bloomed, over 200 is Overgrown. A specimen whose on-chain seed lands at 970 or higher modulo 1000 is a Mutant instead. Forms never change after sealing.

$LAB

$LAB is a fixed-supply token of 10,000,000,000 units, created once with no mint function and no presale. Holders of specimens from sealed experiments claim $LAB once per specimen, paid by final form: 10,000 LAB for Dormant, 50,000 for Bloomed, 250,000 for Overgrown, and 1,000,000 for a Mutant. Each claim carries a flat fee in BNB, set in the LabClaim contract and forwarded to the project wallet in the same transaction, and claiming never burns the specimen. A claim pool of 2,000,000,000 LAB (20% of supply) funds the claims; 8,000,000,000 LAB (80%) sits in the project wallet.

No $LAB liquidity pool exists yet. Swap routes on this site are prefilled and marked Pool · Soon, and they begin working only when a pool is created.
